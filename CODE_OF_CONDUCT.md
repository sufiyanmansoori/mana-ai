# Community standards

Participation in Mana AI discussions and contributions requires respectful,
constructive conduct.

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, and personal
  attacks.
- Respect privacy; do not publish private conversations, credentials, or
  personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and helping its users.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed
to protect contributors. Report concerns privately using the process in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
